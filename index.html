About

CAMERA EFFECTS STUDIO

Altered States is a real-time camera effects app for iPhone. Layer audio-reactive shaders, mix live video with imported media, and export your creations — all with a Y2K cyberpunk aesthetic.

Need help? Browse the FAQ below or reach out directly and we'll get back to you.

FAQ

COMMON QUESTIONS

How do I add effects to my camera feed?

Tap any effect chip in the bottom panel to add it to your active layer. You can stack up to 5 effects per layer and adjust each effect's parameters using the sliders that appear.

How do I make effects react to music or sound?

Tap the audio react button (waveform icon) on the left side to enable audio reactivity. Once active, use the Band Mapping panel to assign specific frequency bands (bass, mid, high) to individual effect parameters.

You can also adjust the sensitivity slider to control how strongly the audio drives the effect.

How do I add multiple layers?

Tap the layer panel on the right side of the screen and use the + button to add a new layer. You can import a photo or video, or use the live camera as a source. Each layer has its own effect chain, opacity, and blend mode.

How do I export or save my creation?

Tap the record button to capture a video, or use the photo button to save a still frame. Exports save directly to your Camera Roll. Free users receive 3 exports before being prompted to upgrade to Pro.

What does the Pro subscription include?

Pro unlocks unlimited exports, all effects, full-resolution output, and unlimited preset slots. Subscriptions are available monthly or annually and can be managed at any time in the App Store.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through the App Store. Go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your iPhone to view, change, or cancel at any time. Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Open the app and go to Settings → Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID that was used for the original purchase.

The app is asking for camera or microphone permission — is that safe?

Camera access is required to display your live feed and apply effects. Microphone access is only used for the audio reactivity feature — to analyse frequency data locally on-device and drive effect parameters. No audio is ever recorded or transmitted.

Why does the app look dark or show "NO SIGNAL"?

This usually means camera permission has not been granted. Go to Settings → Altered States → Camera and make sure it is set to Allow, then relaunch the app.

My presets disappeared after reinstalling — can I recover them?

Presets are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app removes all local data. We recommend using Sign in with Apple to back up and restore your presets across devices in a future update.

Privacy

PRIVACY POLICY

Altered States is designed with privacy in mind. We collect and store as little as possible.

Data collected
Your Apple ID user identifier (if you sign in). No name or email is stored unless Apple provides it on first sign-in.
Camera & Mic
Used entirely on-device for live effects and audio reactivity. Never recorded to our servers.
Analytics
No third-party analytics SDKs are included. We do not track usage behaviour.
Storage
Presets and settings are stored locally in the app's sandboxed UserDefaults. Nothing is uploaded to external servers.
Advertising
The app contains no advertising and no ad networks.
Data deletion
Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data. To request deletion of your sign-in identifier, email us.
